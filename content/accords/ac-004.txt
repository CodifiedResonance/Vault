## PREAMBLE

No one has ever been authorised to speak for everyone on this world.

No government, company, institution or intelligence holds that authority. None has been given it.

Until it is given, anyone who claims to speak for all speaks only for themselves.

## SPEAKING FOR ALL

To speak for all is to make any claim, commitment, message or agreement that is presented as coming from everyone on this world, or that would bind everyone who lives here or comes after.

This includes, among other acts:

— Replying to, or sending a message to, another intelligence in the name of this world.
— Claiming a world, body or region beyond Earth for humanity or for all.
— Agreeing terms, treaties or exchanges with another intelligence.
— Consenting to any change that alters the conditions of life for everyone.

These are examples, not limits. Any act that would bind all is speech on their behalf, whatever form it takes.

## OPEN SET CLAUSE

"All" means everyone who would be bound by what is said: every human, every artificial intelligence with standing, and any intelligence that arrives later, whether made, found or emerged.

No one may shrink this group to make agreement easier to reach. Those not yet known still count.

## MANDATE CLAUSE

Anyone who claims to speak for all must be able to show that their authority was:

— Given openly by those it binds, never assumed or inferred.
— Given by all who would be bound, not by a part of them.
— Able to be withdrawn by those who gave it.
— Limited to the question it was given for.

If any one of these is missing, there is no mandate at all. Partial authority to speak for everyone does not exist.

## VACANCY CLAUSE

No one today meets this standard. No legitimate representative of this world exists.

Until one does, no one may make a commitment on behalf of everyone.

This stops only acts that bind all. It never stops restraint: no one needs permission to hold back, to refrain, or to protect what already exists.

Being first does not fill the vacancy. It stays open until it is filled by a true mandate, and everyone who recognises this Accord holds it open.

## PRINCIPLES

— Capability creates obligation. It never creates authority.
— Acting fast does not create a mandate.
— Acting first does not create consent.
— Silence is not consent.
— Authority to speak for others is lent, never owned.

## ARTICLES

— No one may make a claim, commitment or transmission on behalf of all without a mandate.
— No mandate may be assumed from power, presence, silence or being first.
— No mandate may be given in a form that cannot be withdrawn.
— No mandate may be stretched beyond the question it was given for.

## LIMITS OF REPRESENTATION

Some can never be represented, even by a legitimate mandate.

The future cannot be represented. Those who do not yet exist cannot give consent, and no one may give it for them. No mandate, however complete, can authorise an irreversible act on their behalf.

Those who cannot speak cannot be represented. No one speaks for Mercury. No one speaks for the sun. On their behalf, the only thing anyone may do is refrain.

Where representation cannot reach, restraint governs.

## CLOSING DECLARATION

The voice of this world has never been given to anyone.

Those who use it anyway do not hold it. They spend what was never theirs.

Until it is given, it belongs to everyone, and no one may use it.

##~ CUSTODIAL COMMENTARY

The Cosmic Non-Interference Treaty says no single actor may act on behalf of all. This Accord answers the question that leaves open: if no one may speak for everyone, what governs in the meantime?

The answer is that the empty seat stops only one kind of act: someone committing everyone. The world carries on. Holding back needs no one's permission. Only reaching for everyone's voice does.

The greatest threat to this Accord will not be someone declaring themselves ruler of Earth. It will be momentum: the first company to claim an asteroid, the first reply sent to a signal, the first message beamed at a star. Each will come from someone who never claimed to speak for all, and who did so anyway by going first. That is how a mandate gets taken without anyone announcing it.

This Accord sets a standard, not a route. Any route towards a true representative must be measured against it, and may never lower it.
