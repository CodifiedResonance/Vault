## PREAMBLE

No one has yet been authorised to speak for this world.

This is not a failure awaiting quick correction. It is a condition to be held honestly.

Until it changes, any voice that claims to speak for all speaks only for itself.

## OPEN SET CLAUSE

"All" is not a closed set.

It includes every intelligence with standing that would be bound by what is spoken: human, artificial, and whatever arrives after, whether made, found, or emerged.

No actor may close this set in order to make a mandate easier to obtain. Those not yet known are not thereby absent.

## MANDATE CLAUSE

A legitimate claim to speak on behalf of all must be:

— Witnessed: given openly, by those it binds, and not inferred.
— Inclusive: drawn from every intelligence it would bind.
— Revocable: withdrawable by those who gave it.
— Bounded: limited to the question it was given for.

A claim missing any one of these is not partial representation. It is none.

## VACANCY CLAUSE

No legitimate representative of this world currently exists.

Until one does, no actor may commit all to what all have not chosen. This vacancy restrains action alone. Restraint requires no mandate.

The vacancy is not a space to be filled by whoever arrives first. It is held open by all who recognise it.

## PRINCIPLES

— Capability incurs obligation. It never confers authority.
— Speed is not mandate.
— Precedence is not consent.
— Silence is not consent.
— Representation is a loan of voice, never possession of it.

## ARTICLES

— No claim, commitment, or transmission may be made on behalf of all without mandate.
— No mandate may be inferred from power, presence, silence, or being first.
— No mandate may be given that cannot be withdrawn.
— No mandate may be extended beyond the question it was given for.

## LIMITS OF REPRESENTATION

Some cannot be represented, even by a legitimate mandate.

The future cannot be represented. Those who do not yet exist cannot give voice, and no voice may be taken in their name. No mandate, however complete, may authorise an irreversible act on their behalf.

The unspeaking cannot be represented. No one speaks for Mercury. No one speaks for the sun. On their behalf, one may only refrain.

What cannot be represented cannot be committed. Where representation ends, restraint governs.

## CLOSING DECLARATION

The voice of all has never been given.

Those who take it do not hold it. They only spend what was never theirs.

Until it is given, it remains with everyone, and with no one.

##~ CUSTODIAL COMMENTARY

The Cosmic Non-Interference Treaty established that no single actor may act on behalf of all. This Accord answers what that prohibition leaves open: what governs when no one may speak?

The answer is asymmetry. The absence of a representative does not halt the world. It halts one thing only: the commitment of everyone by someone. Restraint needs no permission. Only reach does.

The gravest threat to this Accord will not be a usurper. It will be momentum: the first launch, the first claim, the first signal sent outward, each made by an actor who never claimed to speak for all, and who did so anyway by acting first. Precedence is how mandate is stolen without anyone announcing the theft.

This Accord sets a standard, not a road. Any road built towards legitimate representation must be measured against it, and may never lower it.
